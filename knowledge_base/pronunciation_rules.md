# ENGLISH PRONUNCIATION (ELEMENTARY) - RAG VERSION

## OVERVIEW
Materi pronunciation dibagi menjadi 4 level utama:
1. Sounds (bunyi dasar)
2. Words (suku kata & stress)
3. Sentences (linking & grammar)
4. Conversation (intonation)

---

# SECTION A: SOUNDS & SPELLING

## BASIC CONCEPT

### Letters vs Sounds
- 26 letters ≠ jumlah sound
- English punya >40 sounds

### Rules:
- 1 letter ≠ 1 sound
- 1 sound bisa banyak spelling
- Ada silent letters

Example:
- green → 5 letters, 4 sounds
- listen → t silent
- six → x = k + s

---

# VOWEL SOUNDS (CORE)

## /i:/ (long i)
Example:
- see, eat, team, key

Characteristics:
- long sound
- mouth wide (smile)

Sentence:
- Can you see the sea?

---

## /ɪ/ (short i)
Example:
- sit, miss, dinner, swim

Characteristics:
- short sound
- mouth slightly open

Sentence:
- Dinner in the kitchen

---

## /u:/ (long u)
Example:

Characteristics:
- lips rounded
- long sound

# CONSONANT SOUNDS (IMPORTANT PAIRS)

## /p/ vs /b/
- /p/ = no voice (air keluar kuat)
- /b/ = ada vibration

Example:
- pen vs ben
- pack vs back

---

## /t/ vs /d/
- /t/ = no voice
- /d/ = voiced

Example:
- ten vs den
- write vs ride

---

## /k/ vs /g/
Example:
- cat vs got
- back vs bag

---

## /f/ vs /v/
Example:
- fan vs van
- leaf vs leave

# KEY PRONUNCIATION RULES

## 1. Stress (TEKANAN)
- penting untuk meaning
- biasanya di syllable tertentu

Example:
- 'record (noun)
- re'cord (verb)

---

## 2. Strong vs Weak Sound
- kata kecil sering jadi lemah

Example:
- and → /ənd/
- to → /tə/

---

## 3. Linking
Kata disambung saat speaking

Example:
- pick it up → /pɪkɪtʌp/

---

## 4. Silent Letters
Example:
- listen (t silent)
- knife (k silent)

---

## 5. Sound Reduction
Example:
- going to → gonna
- want to → wanna
